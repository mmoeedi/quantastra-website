Aircraft Suitability

Can each type physically operate this sector, and does the destination runway support it?

Ranges are nominal published figures; real range trades against payload, so sectors near an aircraft's max are flagged payload-limited. Runway figures are approximate sea-level requirements — always verify against the official AIP and the day's conditions (temperature, elevation, weight).
